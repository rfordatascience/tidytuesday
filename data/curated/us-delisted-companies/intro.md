This week we're exploring what happened to US-listed companies worth $1 billion or more that disappeared from the NYSE, Nasdaq and NYSE American between October 2021 and October 2026. The dataset comes from the [Quotelinks Delisted Stocks Report](https://www.quotelinks.com/delisted-stocks-report.html), with industry codes and the official Form 25 delisting notices added from SEC EDGAR.

> Most did not fail. Of the 473 companies, 283 were bought by another company, 124 were taken private and 36 merged; 25 left US exchanges but kept trading abroad, and 5 left for other reasons. 2025 was the busiest year, with 101 delistings.

Each row is one company, with its outcome, the buyer, how shareholders were paid, the deal terms and value, the announcement and closing dates, a short plain-English summary, and a link to the SEC filing that removed the stock from its exchange.

- Which industries (SIC codes) were most often taken private, and by whom?
- How long did it take deals to close, from announcement to closing, and did that change over time?
- Did all-cash deals dominate in every year, or did all-stock deals come back in some years?
- Who were the most active buyers, and what kinds of companies did private equity firms target?
