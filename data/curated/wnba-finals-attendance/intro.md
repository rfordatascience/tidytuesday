This week we're exploring attendance at the WNBA Finals! The dataset comes from [Full Court Buckets](https://fullcourtbuckets.com/news/2026-wnba-finals-ratings-tracker/), which compiled the published attendance, score and arena for every WNBA Finals game from 2002 through 2025 by hand from each game's official WNBA.com box score. Each game row links to the game record it came from.

> Two CSV files cover every WNBA Finals from 2002 through 2025: 24 series and 93 games. Published attendance is available for 90 of the 93 games.

The three 2020 Finals games were played in the bubble at IMG Academy with no fans admitted, so their attendance is `NA` (not zero). The Finals format also changed over time (best-of-three through 2004, best-of-five from 2005 through 2024 and best-of-seven from 2025), so series totals and per-game averages tell different stories.
