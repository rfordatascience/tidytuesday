|variable |class     |description |
|:--------|:---------|:-----------|
|slug     |character |Id of the plugin; joins zotero_plugins. |
|category |character |A job the plugin is listed under: Reading & PDFs, Citing & writing, AI & summaries, Translation, Organising & tags, Import & metadata, Interface & look, or Files, sync & backup. Assigned by AI from the plugin's name and summary, not by hand. |
|main     |logical   |Whether this is the plugin's main category. A plugin has one main category and up to two more. |
