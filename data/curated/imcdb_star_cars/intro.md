This week we're looking at star cars. The [Internet Movie Cars Database](https://www.imcdb.org/) (IMCDb) has catalogued nearly two million vehicle appearances in more than 94,000 movies and TV shows, and its contributors rate each appearance from one star (a background vehicle) to five stars, meaning "the vehicle is part of the movie". Out of all those appearances, only 1,542 get the full five stars. This dataset has all of them, one row per vehicle and title, with the make and model, the countries where the vehicle was designed and built, and the production it starred in.

Some things to look at:

- Which makes get the most starring roles? And where do the star vehicles come from?
- Do productions star the cars of their own day, or older classics? Compare the vehicle year with the release year.
- Some models keep getting cast: the Ford Mustang has 21 starring roles, the Chevrolet Camaro 11. Which models star in the most titles, and in what kinds of productions?
- The titles span more than a century, from silent-era shorts to series still running. How have the star vehicles changed over the decades, in age, body class, or country of origin?
