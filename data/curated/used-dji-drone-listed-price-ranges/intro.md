This week we're exploring **used DJI drone listed-price ranges**. The dataset
contains 43 aircraft model-level aggregates derived from 251 public Reboot Hub
catalog configurations captured on 7 July 2026. For each model, it reports the
lowest, highest, and median listed price in US dollars, along with coverage and
methodology fields.

These values are **listed prices, not completed-sale prices, inventory claims,
or a market-wide price index**. Bundle contents, controllers, batteries,
accessories, condition grades, and regional availability can all affect a
listing. The source is maintained by Reboot Hub, a commercial drone retailer
and repair company. The release contains no private customer, order, or repair
record data. The public methodology documents the aggregation, exclusions, and
commercial-source boundary, and the archived release has a permanent DOI.

Some questions you can explore:

- Which aircraft models have the widest listed-price ranges, and does that
  coincide with greater configuration coverage?
- How does the median listed price vary across release quarters?
- Which models appear most sensitive to bundle composition?
- How do conclusions change when models with fewer than three tracked
  configurations are excluded?
- Which models are potential outliers after normalizing range width by median
  listed price?
